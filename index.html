Support

teamcentersap@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. No account is required to use the app.

What the app does

  • Focus — ideal diopter, nearest standard strength, comparison table
  • Precision — format, lens, offset, and DOF near/far estimates
  • Viewfinder — split planning overlay with FOV and frame width
  • Lens packages — save setups locally on your device

v1.0 is free. All tools above are included with no purchase. Offset-aware results are prep estimates — verify critical shots during lens prep.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

ACam Viewfinder performs optical planning calculations on your device. Version 1.0 does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or profile information
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in this version
  • No access to camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location

On-device storage

Preferences (such as distance units) and saved lens packages are stored locally on your device using standard iOS local storage. This data stays on your device unless you delete the app or clear it.

Purchases

v1.0 ships with all features unlocked and does not require In-App Purchase. If a future version enables App Store purchases, payment is processed by Apple under Apple’s terms.
